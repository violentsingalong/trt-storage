# Bambu Studio: fuzzy-skin coupon

The three STL cards are geometrically identical except for their recessed foot labels. Fuzzy skin is generated by Bambu Studio and is not stored in an STL.

## Import and arrange

1. Import all three fuzzy-skin coupon STLs as separate objects.
2. Keep them upright as modeled, with several millimetres between cards.
3. Use the intended black PETG and the same wall speeds planned for the case.
4. If bed adhesion is questionable, add a small brim to all three objects rather than changing their geometry.

## Per-object settings

Use **0.40 mm point distance** for all three objects and assign the labeled fuzzy-skin thickness to each card:

| Card label | Fuzzy-skin thickness | Point distance |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.05 | 0.05 mm | 0.40 mm |
| 0.08 | 0.08 mm | 0.40 mm |
| 0.12 | 0.12 mm | 0.40 mm |

Apply fuzzy skin to the exterior contour. Confirm in Preview that each broad vertical face receives texture. The feet and labels are not evaluation surfaces.

## Evaluation

Compare the cards under both direct and grazing light for:

- layer-line masking;
- visual uniformity;
- tactile roughness and grip;
- tendency to collect lint;
- corner definition;
- preference at normal viewing distance.

Record the selected thickness together with filament, nozzle, layer height, wall speed, and Bambu Studio profile. Do not apply fuzzy skin to lid fits, stack registration, bezel bearing surfaces, magnet interfaces, or interior storage surfaces.
