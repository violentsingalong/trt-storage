# Supply dimension envelopes

This document separates published product dimensions, photo-derived layout estimates, and measurements still required from the user's actual supplies. All dimensions are millimetres.

## Alcohol prep pads

The controlling dimension for storage is the sealed foil pouch, not the unfolded wipe.

Published examples show a common pouch size of approximately **50 × 50 mm**:

- Wuxi Medical lists 50 × 50 mm foil packaging for common 65 × 30 mm and 65 × 60 mm pads.
- Other manufacturers list 50 × 45 mm and 65 × 65 mm pouch variants.
- Pouch thickness varies with pad ply, fluid load, seal, and trapped air and should be measured as a compressed stack rather than inferred from one packet.

Reference links:

- https://wuxi-medical.com/alcohol-swabs-manufacturer/
- https://chinamedica.com/product/alcohol-prep-pad/
- https://pdi-intl.com/products/prevantics-alcohol-prep-pad/

### Provisional compartment envelope

For common 50 × 50 mm packets, start with **54 × 55 mm usable X/Y** per bay, plus finger relief. This is consistent with the two upper packet stacks visible in the scaled Mega reference photograph, but remains `INFERRED` until actual packets are measured.

- Standard Mega: two bays of roughly 30 packets each.
- Cycle Mega: one deeper central bay may hold roughly 60 packets.
- Do not estimate capacity from single-packet thickness. Measure a lightly compressed stack of 10 and divide by 10, then validate a full intended stack.

## Syringes

Syringe dimensions vary substantially with barrel design, plunger position, tip style, attached needle, needle length, and protective cap. Volume alone is not enough to define a compartment.

The original loaded Mega reference shows loose capped syringe/needle assemblies rather than unopened sterile retail pouches. Photo rectification against the known 215.9 × 152.4 mm exterior suggests a lower usable bay of approximately **155 × 88 mm**, with several assemblies nested diagonally or longitudinally. Treat this as an `INFERRED` layout estimate with approximately ±3 mm uncertainty.

Official capacity claims provide quantity, not object dimensions:

- TRT Vault Mega: 12–20 syringes depending on syringe size.
- Cycle Vault Mega: 20–40 syringes depending on syringe size.

Reference links:

- https://www.trtvault.com/products/trt-vault-mega-anodized-black-1
- https://www.trtvault.com/products/cycle-vault-mega-anodized-black

### Provisional compartment envelope

Use **160 × 90 mm usable X/Y** as the first parametric syringe-bay envelope for Mega/Cycle studies. This is not yet a production dimension. The final bay must be driven by the longest stored 1 mL and 3 mL assemblies and the desired quantity.

Do not assume packaged syringes fit this envelope. Individually sterile pouches are typically much larger than loose capped assemblies and require a separate layout.

## Individually wrapped 1-inch Luer-lock needles

Needles should be stored in their original sealed wrappers. The controlling object is therefore the **sterile package**, not the bare 1-inch needle or its protective cap. Catalogs confirm that sterile, single-use 1-inch Luer-compatible needles are commonly available in multiple gauges, but the reviewed manufacturer pages do not publish individual wrapper dimensions.

Reference links:

- https://www.bd.com/en-us/products-and-solutions/products/product-page.305127
- https://static.bd.com/assets/product/Documents/Medication%20Delivery%20Solutions%20(MDS)/Needles%20and%20Syringes/BD-14837%20(04-23)%20Needles%20and%20Syringes%20Catalog_WEB.pdf

### Compartment requirements

- Keep wrappers flat or gently stacked; do not force tight bends into the sealed edges.
- Provide finger access so a package can be lifted without scraping or pinching its sterile barrier.
- Use radiused divider edges and avoid a tight press fit.
- Size for the largest wrapper among the gauges/brands that will actually be stored.
- Treat a shallow removable divider or dedicated flat bay as preferable to fitted slots for each needle.
- Do not assign a production envelope until an actual package and stack have been measured.

## Measurements required from current supplies

Measure each 1 mL and 3 mL configuration exactly as it will be stored:

| Measurement | What to include |
|---|---|
| Overall stored length | Plunger position, attached needle, and protective cap |
| Maximum width | Finger flange or safety mechanism, whichever is wider |
| Maximum thickness | Barrel, flange, cap, or safety mechanism |
| Barrel diameter | Useful for nesting and divider geometry |
| Needle/cap length | Record separately if needles may be stored detached |
| Sterile pouch X/Y | Only if supplies will remain packaged |
| Quantity | Needed to validate bay width, depth, and stacking arrangement |

For individually wrapped 1-inch Luer-lock needles, measure:

- sealed wrapper length and width, including the seal flange;
- maximum thickness at the capped needle hub;
- lightly compressed height of a stack of 10;
- all gauge/brand variants likely to be stored;
- desired stored quantity.

For alcohol pads, measure:

- sealed pouch X and Y;
- maximum seal-edge thickness if unusually stiff;
- compressed height of a stack of 10;
- desired stored quantity.
