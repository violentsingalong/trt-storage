# Fuzzy-skin coupon V1 results

Material: PETG.

Three minimal upright cards compared a fixed 0.40 mm point distance with fuzzy-skin thicknesses of 0.05, 0.08, and 0.12 mm.

## Result

- The 0.12 mm thickness produced the preferred fuzzy appearance.
- All fuzzy variants showed more surface artifacts than the normal smooth PETG finish.
- The texture did not provide enough cosmetic benefit to offset the additional artifacts.
- **Decision: use a normal smooth finish for production PETG parts.**

Smooth surfaces preserve the option to correct isolated rough areas after printing. Any sanding test should be performed on scrap first because sanding PETG can change gloss, haze the surface, soften edges, and alter functional dimensions. Do not sand stack registration, lid fits, magnet interfaces, vial wells, or bezel bearing surfaces unless correcting a specifically measured fit problem.

The fuzzy-skin CAD, STLs, and Bambu Studio instructions remain in the repository as calibration history. They are not the production profile.
